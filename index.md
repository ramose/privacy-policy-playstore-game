## Data we collect

[GAME NAME] itself collects **no personal information**. No accounts,
no analytics, no tracking of your behavior by us.

The game shows ads via **Google AdMob**. AdMob's SDK automatically
collects certain data from your device to serve and measure ads:

- **Device identifiers** — your Google Advertising ID
- **Device information** — device model, operating system version, app version
- **Approximate location** — derived from your IP address (city-level, not precise)
- **Ad interaction data** — which ads were shown and whether they were tapped

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
More detail on what AdMob collects:
[AdMob & data disclosure](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

## Your choices

- **Opt out of personalized ads:** Android Settings → Privacy → Ads →
  turn off "Ad personalization." You'll still see ads, but they won't be
  tailored to you.
- **Reset your Advertising ID:** same screen → "Reset advertising ID."

## Data stored on your device

The game may keep small settings on your own device (e.g. sound on/off,
high score). This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game
removes it.

## Children

[GAME NAME] is a general-audience game and is not directed at children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided
personal information through the game, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].
